THE STORY

A Kingdom on the Edge

Althea

Adventurers, labyrinths, demons, strange magic, and forgotten bloodlines collide in the kingdom of Pandemonium. Inari Myoko is pulled deeper into a conflict that reaches far beyond the borders of Althea.

“Every region has a secret. Every secret has a price.”

CAST

Main Characters

INARI

Inari Myoko

Mage and central hero of the story. Scarred, determined, and tied to secrets buried deep in Pandemonium.

UMI

Umi

A fierce fighter known for explosive close-range combat, including a flame roundhouse and brutal left hook.

INICHI

Inichi

A sharp-minded magic user with glasses and control over wind-based abilities.

KAJI

Kaji

A skilled fighter from Niccodemia, carrying a bow staff and a calm but dangerous presence.

JADAMI

Jadami Moro

A major figure connected to Opal Kapoi, its castle, and the power struggles surrounding the region.

NERA

Nera Vega

Connected to Bodara and its duty to keep Burnsdat Woods from spreading chaos into nearby lands.
